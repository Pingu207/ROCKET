# TVC Rocket CAD 

CAD files for my thrust vector control (TVC) model rocket, designed in PA.

## What is this?

This is the 3D model of my rocket. TVC means the motor can tilt (called gimbaling) to steer the rocket while it flies, kind of like balancing a broom on your hand. A flight computer reads the sensors and tells the motor mount which way to tilt.

## Parts

 What it does

 Nose cone cuts through the air. 3D printed. 
 Body tube main airframe, LOC Precision cardboard tube, 5.39 in wide. 
 PCB holder holds my flight computer PCB (ESP32 + gyro) in place inside the body. 
 TVC gimbal mount holds the motor and tilts it to steer. 
 Bottom plate | Closes off the bottom of the rocket. 
## Specs

- **Diameter:** 5.39 in
- **Motor:** G-class
- **Materials:** LOC Precision cardboard body tube, 3D-printed nose cone, PCB holder, and gimbal mount
